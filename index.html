What I work with

I work with people dealing with chronic stress, fatigue, digestive issues, hormone imbalance, sleep disruption, and nervous system overload.

These symptoms are often connected. My role is to help identify patterns beneath them and support the body’s ability to regulate over time.

How I work

My work is grounded, methodical, and individualized. I take time to listen, assess patterns, and adjust treatment based on real response — not protocols alone.

Whether in person or remotely, the process is collaborative and paced. We’re not chasing symptoms. We’re restoring function.
