What I Work With

Most people I see don’t have just one isolated problem.

Sleep, energy, digestion, stress, hormones, and metabolism are all connected. When one system is off, it shows up as different symptoms across the body, even when they seem unrelated.

I focus on what’s driving those patterns so things actually shift, not just temporarily improve.

  • Feeling wired but exhausted, or constantly on edge
  • Hormone shifts, cycle irregularities, PMS, or mood swings
  • Bloating, food sensitivities, or sluggish digestion
  • Trying different diets, supplements, or protocols without lasting results
I focus on understanding why symptoms are happening and restoring the systems behind them.

How I Work

My approach is simple. I look at what’s driving your symptoms and work to correct it.

Most people I see have already tried different diets, supplements, or protocols. Sometimes they help, but things don’t fully resolve.

Over time, patterns become clear. I focus on how your body is functioning and how it responds, then adjust as needed so things keep moving forward.

Whether we work together in person or remotely, this is a collaborative process.

1. Start

We begin with a focused intake and conversation to understand what’s happening, what you’ve tried, and what patterns your body may be showing.

2. Plan

From there, I build a clear plan that may include acupuncture, herbal support, nutrition, and lifestyle changes that actually move the needle.

3. Adjust

We track response and refine as needed. The aim is a body that feels more stable, resilient, and able to handle real life again.
