Work With Me

What I Work With

I work with people dealing with chronic stress, fatigue, digestive issues, hormone imbalance, sleep disruption, and nervous system overload.

These symptoms are often connected. My role is to help identify patterns beneath them and support the body’s ability to regulate over time.

  • Chronic stress and nervous system dysregulation
  • Hormonal and thyroid-related symptoms
  • Digestive and metabolic issues
  • Fatigue, burnout, and persistent tension
I focus on understanding why symptoms are happening — not just managing them.

How I Work

My work is grounded, methodical, and individualized. I take time to listen, assess patterns, and adjust treatment based on real response.

We’re not chasing symptoms. We’re restoring function.
