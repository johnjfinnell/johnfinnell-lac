What I Work With

Most people I see don’t have just one isolated problem.

Sleep, energy, digestion, stress, hormones, and metabolism are all deeply interconnected. Symptoms that seem unrelated are often being driven by the same underlying imbalance in the body.

Instead of treating symptoms one by one, I focus on the systems driving them. When those systems improve, seemingly unrelated symptoms often improve together.

  • Chronic stress and nervous system dysregulation
  • Hormonal imbalances and menstrual cycle irregularities
  • Digestive issues and metabolic dysfunction
  • Fatigue, burnout, and low energy
I focus on understanding why symptoms are happening — and restoring the systems behind them.

How I Work

My work is grounded, methodical, and individualized. I listen carefully, assess patterns, and make decisions based on how your body is responding.

Many modern approaches rely heavily on lab testing and treating numbers. Labs can be useful, but they often lead to long supplement protocols aimed at individual markers rather than restoring the systems in trouble.

With enough clinical exposure, clear patterns begin to emerge without relying on extensive testing.

Whether we work together in person or remotely, this is a collaborative process.

1. Start

We begin with a focused intake and conversation to understand what’s happening, what you’ve tried, and what patterns your body may be showing.

2. Plan

From there, I build a clear plan that may include acupuncture, herbal support, nutrition, and lifestyle changes that actually move the needle.

3. Adjust

We track response and refine as needed. The aim is a body that feels more stable, resilient, and able to handle real life again.

About
